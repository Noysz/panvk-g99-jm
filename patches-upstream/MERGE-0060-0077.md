`0015`-`0059` + funnymdzz kbase backend + his phases 8.5-8.16

## Ported into hafiz's `jm/panvk_vX_gpu_queue.c`

* `0069`: `pan_env_flag` (value `0` = off) for `PANVK_KBASE_SYNC_SUBMIT`,
  `_WAIT_SUBMIT`, `_CPU_FENCE_WAIT`, `_CPU_FENCE_EXPORT`, `_FENCE_TRACE`,
  `_CTRL_NO_FENCE_WAIT_DEP`, `_CTRL_EXPORT_EARLY`, `_NO_SIGNAL`,
  `_EVENT_NO_SET`.
* `0070`: `panvk_mem_prof_tick()` once per `vkQueueSubmit`.
* `0072`: MALLOC_VERTEX `Draw.vertex_array` reset in his re-issue block
  (after his wait for the batch's previous run). `PANVK_V9_NO_REISSUE_VA_RESET=1`.
* `0076`/`0077`: per-atom record (command buffer, batch, index, chain) at
  submit, `PANVK_FAULT_REPORT=1` report and pointer audit from his event
  handler, `PANVK_FAULT_AUDIT_ALL=1` before each batch. State lives in his
  `struct panvk_kbase_jm` (under its mutex).
* Failure log: his handler printed every failed atom; it now prints the
  first 16 and then one summary line per second (Tomb Raider fails about
  1000 chains per second after its first fault on test5).

## New on top of the merge (test7)

* `PANVK_FRAME_PROF=1|<file>` (`panvk_frame_prof.{c,h}`): one line per 2 s
  with GPU busy % (union of [submit, completion seen] of every kbase
  submission), process CPU % (`/proc/self/stat`), time in `vkQueueSubmit`,
  time threads waited for the GPU, submits and passes. With
  `PANVK_KBASE_SYNC_SUBMIT=1` also the wall time of vertex/tiler/compute
  vs fragment chains. Frames are counted in a `panvk_QueuePresentKHR`
  wrapper around `wsi_QueuePresentKHR`; inside Winlator the Wrapper layer
  presents by itself, so the log says "no presents seen" there and reports
  per second.
* hafiz's queue read `PANVK_KBASE_SYNC_SUBMIT`, `PANVK_HEAP_LOG` and
  `PANVK_KBASE_NO_SIGNAL` on every `vkQueueSubmit`; they are read once at
  queue creation now.

## Fixes needed on the new base (not textual conflicts)

* `panvk_vX_shader.c`, our GS rasterization variant: the 5-argument
  `pan_varying_collect_formats()` and `trust_varying_flat_highp_types` are
  gone upstream (`e0907dc5`, `b2c14af8`): 3-argument call, as hafiz did on
  the TES path. Compile error.
* `src/compiler/nir/nir_gather_info.c`: the software vertex shader variant
  (v9 tessellation and geometry through libpoly; CSF-only and untested in
  hafiz's tree, enabled on v9 by our `0062`/`0063`) lowers its inputs to
  `load_attr_pan` and then gathers info. Upstream lists `load_attr_pan`
  among the input loads but `nir_get_io_offset_src()` has no offset source
  for it, so the gather dereferenced NULL: every tessellation, geometry and
  adjacency/patch-list pipeline crashed (first regression run: 141 tess,
  90 GS, 10 topology, 2 XFB cases). The gather now uses the handle
  (`src[2]`) for it.

aborted at its first `vkDestroyDevice` on
`vk_pipeline_cache_destroy: cache->object_cache->entries == 0`.
