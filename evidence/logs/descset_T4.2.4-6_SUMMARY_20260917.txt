CORRECTION to the first reading of T4.2.4:
  The 0/4096 was initially read as 'geometry died'. That was wrong.
  T4.2.6 shows 512/4096 under the same skip=1 condition once one channel
  is descriptor-independent. The triangle rasterizes normally in both
  cases; in T4.2.4 all three of its colour channels came from zeroed
  descriptors, so it painted 0,0,0,255 - identical to the clear colour and
  therefore invisible to a non-black pixel counter.
  The constant channel is what makes the two failure modes separable.

SCOPE LIMIT:
  Geometry survives here because this vertex shader uses gl_VertexIndex
  with a hardcoded position array - no vertex attributes, no vertex
  buffers - so it needs nothing from its resource table, and its viewport
  constants arrive via FAU push uniforms instead. A workload with real
  vertex buffers is NOT covered by this result.
